A ball holder that clamps onto a sports wheelchair. Watch the cup slide on and off.

Open this page on a phone or tablet to see it in your room.

  1. Tap the button, then allow the camera.
  2. Point at the floor or a table and move your phone slowly.
  3. Pinch to make it bigger or smaller. Drag to move it.
